## ⚡ 5 Daily Tasks You Can Do in Seconds

### 1. Mark a Dish "Sold Out Today" (1 Tap)
- **When:** Kitchen 86s an item (e.g. fresh seafood or daily special).
- **How:** Go to **Menu** → find the dish → tap the toggle **"Sold Out Today"**.
- **Result:** Dish instantly greys out on all customer phones with a calm "Sold out for today" badge. Prevents guest disappointment at the table.
- **Tip:** Items reset automatically the next morning, or you can untoggle manually anytime.

### 2. Update Prices or Run a Bulk Adjustment
- **Single Dish:** Tap the dish in **Menu** → edit the price field → tap **Save**.
- **Bulk Adjust (Inflation / Seasonal change):**
  - Go to **Menu** → tap **Bulk Price Adjust**.
  - Choose **Percentage** (e.g. `+5%`) or **Fixed Amount** (e.g. `+$1.00`).
  - Select categories (e.g. "Beverages" or "All Dishes") → tap **Apply Bulk Update**.

### 3. Create a Flash Offer or Happy Hour (30 Seconds)
- **When:** Slow Tuesday evening or weekday Happy Hour.
- **How:** Go to **Offers** → tap **New Offer**.
- **Choose Type:**
  - **Happy Hour:** e.g. 20% off Cocktails between 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM.
  - **Chef's Deal:** Fixed price special on a signature combo.
- **Urgency:** The system automatically calculates honest time remaining (e.g. "Ends in 45 mins") without fake countdowns.

### 4. Respond to Dish Reviews
- **When:** A guest rates a dish 1–5 stars and leaves a note.
- **How:** Go to **Reviews** → tap **Reply** on any review.
- **Reply Etiquette:**
  - Thank happy diners for their compliments.
  - If a dish was "Too salty" or "Portion small", acknowledge it warmly and invite them back to try the chef's revised preparation.
- **Note:** Reviews only show publicly on a dish once it has received at least 3 reviews to protect new menu items.

### 5. Print Fresh Table QR Stands
- **When:** A table tent gets damaged or you add outdoor patio tables.
- **How:** Go to **More (⋯)** → **QR Studio** → select the table number → tap **Print Stand / PDF**.
- **Rule:** Minimum print size is **4 cm (1.6 inches)** for table stands. Always print on **matte** cardstock to ensure instant camera scanning under restaurant lights.
